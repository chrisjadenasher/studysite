# Exam Desk

Free tools for Cambridge IGCSE, AS & A Level students:

- **Grade thresholds**: compare your paper marks with every Cambridge threshold table from March 2020 to June 2026, see what you need on papers you haven't sat yet, and get safe target totals.
- **PUM calculator**: convert a percentage uniform mark into the raw total it needed in any series.
- **Exam timer**: official paper lengths, with pause, reset, warnings and extra time.
- **Periodic table**: element properties, plus group and period trends for the 0620 and 9701 syllabuses.
- **Practical toolkit**: uncertainties, significant figures and gradients for Papers 3 and 5.
- **Exam countdown**: your papers in date order, filled in from Cambridge's timetable.

The site is in `public/`. It publishes to GitHub Pages automatically on every push to `main` (see `.github/workflows/pages.yml`). Optional Google sign-in uses Firebase; see `SETUP.md`.

Threshold figures are copied from Cambridge International's published grade threshold tables. Grades shown are estimates; your real grade comes only from Cambridge. This site is not affiliated with Cambridge University Press & Assessment.
